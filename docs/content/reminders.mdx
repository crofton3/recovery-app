# Reminders

Reminders are local notifications that the app schedules on your phone. There's no push server, so they work with a free Apple ID.

- **Workouts:** one reminder at each scheduled session's time, for the next 14 days.
- **Achilles check:** a nudge at 8:00 PM each day.
- Today's reminders are skipped once you finish the workout or log the check.
- The list is rebuilt every time you open the app or change your schedule.

Because they're local, a reminder only exists if you've opened the app within the last 14 days. Open it now and then, which you'll do anyway to log your sets.
