# Design

The look is called Recovery Aurora: a dark, Apple-style design with activity rings, large numerals and soft glows.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#070B14` | Screens |
| Card | `#0F1626` | Cards and the tab bar |
| Text | `#EAF0FF` | Text and numerals |
| Muted text | `#7B88A8` | Secondary text, 12pt and up |
| Recovery | `#00F5A0` | WHOOP recovery, primary buttons, done sets |
| Strain | `#00B4FF` | WHOOP strain, training |
| Sleep | `#7C5CFF` | Sleep graphics (sleep text uses `#A593FF`) |
| Nutrition | `#FFB547` | Calories, protein, PR badges |
| Water | `#4DE8FF` | Water, swelling |
| Alert | `#FF6B81` | Pain and low days, always with a word |

## Rules

- One colour per metric on every screen.
- Rings have round caps, a faint track and a glow. They fill with a spring, and Reduce Motion shows them filled.
- Touch targets are at least 44pt and text is at least 12pt.
- Status is never colour alone.

The tokens are in `ios/AchillesRecovery/Design/Theme.swift`.
