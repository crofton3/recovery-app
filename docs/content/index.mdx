# Achilles

A free iPhone app for tracking recovery from Achilles surgery: rehab phase, milestones, pain and swelling, strength and range of motion, lifting, food, and WHOOP data, all in one place.

It's a personal project. It's built for one person (me) on one iPhone, with no subscription and no accounts.

## What's in it

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| Today | WHOOP recovery, strain and sleep rings, calories, protein and water, today's workout, and a daily pain and swelling check. |
| Train | A session logger with sets, weight and reps, last-time values and PR badges, plus a weekly schedule with planned sets and reps. |
| Achilles | Rehab phase, milestone checklist, pain and swelling trend, left and right strength and range of motion, and a PT session log. |
| Fuel | Manual food entry, quick add, an end-of-day total, and daily targets. |
| Trends | WHOOP recovery, sleep and strain charts, recovery against training days, and body weight and waist. |

## How it fits together

- **iPhone app:** SwiftUI, iOS 17 and up.
- **Database:** your own free Supabase project (Postgres).
- **WHOOP:** two Supabase Edge Functions keep your WHOOP login on the server and copy your data into the database.
- **Reminders:** local notifications on the phone. No push server.
